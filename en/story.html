Story

The Vindicators were born in Bassano del Grappa in 1986, right after Frigidaire Tango broke up. Some of its members started a project “carefree and aimed at pure fun”: rock'n'roll, garage and rhythm & blues. The name is a nod to The Fleshtones, the New York band that inspired their show.

  1. 1986

    The band is born. They play live: theatres, squares and clubs between Bassano and Vicenza.

  2. Christmas 1987

    That's All Rock comes out, recorded and mixed at Art Music Studio in Bassano. Keith Streng and Bill Milhizer of The Fleshtones take part in the sessions. It sells out in six months and is reissued in 1988 with a new cover.

  3. 1989

    The line-up changes and Roots Revolution follows, on C.G.D. A long tour from Trento to Naples comes next, with opening slots for the Blues Brothers; the band are guests of honour at Arezzo Wave.

  4. 1992

    The Vindicators split after 102 shows. Over the years they worked with members of The Fleshtones and The Fuzztones.

  5. 2012

    They return with Greatest Hits (Go Down Records), a double CD with the best of both albums, four unreleased tracks and a live disc. The music press welcomes it with enthusiastic reviews. Read the press.

  6. 31 October 2026

    On stage at the Capitol in Pordenone, opening the night for The Fleshtones. All the details.

Frequently asked questions

Who are The Vindicators?

The Vindicators are a rock'n'roll, garage and rhythm & blues band from Bassano del Grappa, Italy, formed in 1986. They released three records and played 102 shows between 1986 and 1992, and are playing live again.

How did the band start?

In 1986, right after Frigidaire Tango split, some of those musicians started a project «carefree and aimed at pure fun». The name is a tribute to The Fleshtones, the New York band that inspired their show.

Which records have they released?

That's All Rock (1987, recorded at Art Music Studio in Bassano), Roots Revolution (1989, for C.G.D.) and the double CD Greatest Hits (2012, Go Down Records). Tracklists and listening are on the Records page.

Who has played in the band?

Charli Out Cazale (vocals), Stewie DC (guitar) and Paul Milanì (sax) since 1986; Fernando Viktor (bass) and Mark Charles (drums) joined in 1989. The full line-up is on The band page.

Where can I listen to The Vindicators?

On this site you can play the songs from That's All Rock and Roots Revolution on the Records page; Greatest Hits is on Spotify and many videos are in the band's YouTube playlist.

Sources: Rockit, Metallus, the band's own presentation.
