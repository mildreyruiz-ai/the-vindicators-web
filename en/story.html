Story

The Vindicators were born in Bassano del Grappa in 1986, right after Frigidaire Tango broke up. Some of its members started a project “carefree and aimed at pure fun”: rock'n'roll, garage and rhythm & blues. The name is a nod to The Fleshtones, the New York band that inspired their show.

Timeline

A story hung on a string, click a polaroid to enlarge it.

  1. 1986

    The band is born. They play live: theatres, squares and clubs between Bassano and Vicenza.

  2. Christmas 1987

    That's All Rock comes out, recorded and mixed at Art Music Studio in Bassano, with Keith Streng and Bill Milhizer of the Fleshtones. It sells out in six months and is reissued in 1988 with a different cover.

  3. 1988

    That's All Rock is reissued with a different cover after the first pressing sells out.

  4. 1989

    The line-up changes and Roots Revolution arrives, on Casal Gajardo Records. A long tour from Trento to Naples, opening for the Blues Brothers; guests of honour at Arezzo Wave.

  5. 1992

    The Vindicators split up after 102 shows. Over the years they worked with members of the Fleshtones and the Fuzztones.

  6. 2001

    17–19 August: three nights in Bassano with Keith Streng of the Fleshtones, at Shindy Club, Underbar Pacelli and Monte Cruz. A live album comes out in a limited edition of 10 copies.

  7. 2011

    16 December: all the members get together for a group photo, a year before the comeback.

  8. 2012

    They return with Greatest Hits (Go Down Records) and a summer of shows: Vedelago, Asolo, Padua and, on 7 September in Bassano, in the square with Giuliano Palma & The Bluebeaters.

  9. 2013

    6 December at Vinile in Rosà, with the Fleshtones as special guests from New York.

  10. 2014

    3 December: back on stage at Vinile in Rosà.

  11. 2016

    A year on stage: Quinto di Treviso, Giavera del Montello, Roncade (New Age, a David Bowie tribute night), Fossalta (Red Moon Fest) and in December Bassano and Montebelluna.

  12. 2019

    31 May: a show at the Castello degli Ezzelini in Bassano; on 20 July in Crespano del Grappa.

  13. 2022

    18 June in Crespano del Grappa, 20 July in Piazza Terraglio in Bassano.

  14. 2024

    18 May: a night in Crespano del Grappa.

  15. 2026

    Rehearsals at ArtMusic Studio. On 31 October on stage at the Capitol in Pordenone, opening for the Fleshtones. Full details.

Frequently asked questions

Who are The Vindicators?

The Vindicators are a rock'n'roll, garage and rhythm & blues band from Bassano del Grappa, Italy, formed in 1986. They released three records and played 102 shows between 1986 and 1992, and are playing live again.

How did the band start?

In 1986, right after Frigidaire Tango split, some of those musicians started a project «carefree and aimed at pure fun». The name is a tribute to The Fleshtones, the New York band that inspired their show.

Which records have they released?

That's All Rock (1987, recorded at Art Music Studio in Bassano), Roots Revolution (1989, for Casal Gajardo Records) and the double CD Greatest Hits (2012, Go Down Records). Tracklists and listening are on the Records page.

Who has played in the band?

Charli Out Cazale (vocals), Stewie DC (guitar) and Paul Milanì (sax) since 1986; Fernando Viktor (bass) and Mark Charles (drums) joined in 1989. The full line-up is on The band page.

Where can I listen to The Vindicators?

On this site you can play the songs from That's All Rock and Roots Revolution on the Records page; Greatest Hits is on Spotify and many videos are in the band's YouTube playlist.

Sources: Rockit, Metallus, the band's own presentation.
