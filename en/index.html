The Capitol in Pordenone: a reunion of friends and a packed room

On Saturday 31 October 2026 The Vindicators opened the night for The Fleshtones on the stage of the Capitol in Pordenone, on the Italian date of the American band's fifty years of career.

It was a reunion of musicians who have been friends for almost thirty years, welcomed by a loyal audience that filled the room. Thank you to everyone who was there, to The Fleshtones and to the Capitol.

Who we are

Since 1986, for pure fun

The Vindicators were born in Bassano del Grappa in 1986, right after Frigidaire Tango broke up, as a project “carefree and aimed at pure fun”: rock'n'roll, garage and rhythm & blues. The name is a nod to The Fleshtones, who inspired their show.

Three records, 102 shows from Trento to Naples, opening slots for the Blues Brothers and a comeback in 2012. The stage has always been their strength.
