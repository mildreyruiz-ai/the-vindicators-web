Mark Brenda

live 1986 → 1996 · in memoriam

Mark Brenda played live with The Vindicators from 1986 to 1996, then left the band. He did not take part in the recording of That's All Rock, but he plays on Roots Revolution. He has passed away; we remember him with affection.

1989

Charli Out Cazale, Stewie DC, Paul Milanì and Mark Brenda stay. Two new musicians join for the album Roots Revolution and the tour that follows.

Before The Vindicators

Frigidaire Tango

Several of them come from Frigidaire Tango, the Bassano band that split in 1986. A song links the two projects: Don't Kill Time, left out by Frigidaire Tango, ended up on Roots Revolution.
