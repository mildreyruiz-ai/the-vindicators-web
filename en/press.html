Press

What the music press wrote when The Vindicators came back with Greatest Hits. “Lunga vita ai The Vindicators” (long live The Vindicators), In Your Eyes concludes.

From the magazines

Greatest Hits reviews · 2012 · click a clipping to enlarge it

Rumore

by Barbara Tomasino · 2012

Celebrates an unleashed rock'n'roll, a double record with the best of both albums plus a live set, and singles out Charli Out Cazale's voice and a version of Can't Take My Eyes Off You.

Rockerilla

by Aldo Chimenti · 2012

Calls it a necessary, unmissable revival and values the live disc, with 18 songs recorded on stage.

Il Venerdì di Repubblica — Cult

unsigned · 2012

A short review presenting the band as large and loud, explaining the record's title and recommending it to fans of The Fuzztones and Frigidaire Tango.

Il Mucchio Selvaggio

by Aurelio Pagini · 2012

Sees in The Vindicators a change of course from Frigidaire Tango, towards the roots of rock'n'roll, R&B, beat and blues, and praises the original material and the live set.

Blow Up — Mod Revival

by Paolo Bertoni · 2012

Places the record in the 80s revival and highlights the unreleased tracks, the live second CD and the guest spot of The Fleshtones' Streng and Milhizer on All Around the World.
