Privacy and cookies

How thevindicators.it handles your data.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This is the official website of the band The Vindicators (thevindicators.it). The data controller is the band itself. For anything about your personal data you can write to info@thevindicators.it.

What this site does not do

The site has no user accounts, comments, newsletter or online shop. It does not use advertising or profiling cookies.

Contacting us

The contact form does not send anything to the website: when you press Send, it opens your own email app with a message addressed to info@thevindicators.it. If you email us, we use your name, address and message only to reply, and keep them for as long as the conversation needs.

Hosting and technical data

The site is delivered through Cloudflare. Like any web server, it may process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, only to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Visit statistics

Only if you accept in the cookie notice, we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited) to learn, in aggregate, how many people visit the site and which pages, songs and videos interest them most. Until you accept, the tool is not loaded and sets no cookies. If you accept, Google sets its own statistics cookies (_ga and _ga_*, for up to two years) and receives technical data such as your IP address, device type and pages viewed, which may also be processed outside the European Union. We do not use this data for advertising. You can change your mind at any time: review your cookie choice.

Third-party content

Some content is loaded from other services, which may receive your IP address and set their own cookies under their own policies: Google Fonts (typefaces), YouTube (video thumbnails and the player, loaded only when you press a video) and Spotify (the player on the records page). Links to YouTube, Spotify and other sites take you away from this site, and their own privacy policies apply from that point.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies of its own. Only with your consent, the Google Analytics statistics cookies described above are set. Your choice is remembered in your browser (vn_consent). The third-party services above may set their own. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, portability, and you can object to processing. Write to info@thevindicators.it. You also have the right to complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Changes

If something changes in how the site works, this page will be updated.
